Trust & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to NOVA AI ("we," "our," or "us"), founded and engineered by Rohan Mondal. We provide an advanced, multi-modal productivity command center integrating streaming conversational intelligence, automated multi-page website synthesis, daily planning tools, and real-time cloud synchronization.

Your privacy is central to our architectural design. We operate with a strict privacy-first model: your data belongs to you, and your confidential prompts, documents, and code are never sold, rented, or repurposed to train third-party public foundation models.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only as necessary to provide, synchronize, and safeguard your workspace:

  • Account Information: When you register or authenticate via Google OAuth or email, we receive your email address, display name, and optional avatar URL. Passwords created on NOVA AI are cryptographically salted and hashed using bcryptjs before storage and are never visible to anyone.
  • Workspace Content: User-generated content including conversational prompts, chat histories, synthesized multi-file website projects, generated documents, and custom productivity schedules.
  • Technical & Session Telemetry: Anonymized HTTP request logs, browser type, operating system version, and client-side performance metrics required to ensure zero-latency responsiveness and thwart malicious traffic.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To dispatch your prompts to AI inference engines (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, Pollinations AI) and stream responses back in real time.
  • To persist and synchronize your chats, website code, and memories across your devices using our Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL database.
  • To authenticate sessions, issue tamper-proof JSON Web Tokens (JWT), and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To protect our infrastructure from automated bots, spam injections, and denial-of-service attempts.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

NOVA AI interfaces with trusted, enterprise-grade cloud service providers:

  • Supabase Inc.: Provides our primary cloud PostgreSQL database and Google OAuth gateway with data encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Google Cloud & Gemini API: Processes multi-turn conversational chat, creative documents, and code synthesis via secure, stateless API calls. Google does not use customer API inputs to train foundation models.
  • Vercel Inc.: Hosts our serverless edge functions and delivers static assets with global CDN caching and SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Pollinations AI: Synthesizes high-resolution imagery and visual assets on demand based on specific creative prompts.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

NOVA AI does not use invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers. We utilize only essential browser technologies:

  • localStorage: Retains your encrypted session token (novaAuthToken), active conversation identifier, interface theme preferences, and offline fallback cache to guarantee zero-lockout continuity when offline.
  • Essential Session Cookies: Facilitates OAuth handshake handoffs when logging in via Google.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, NOVA AI guarantees full sovereignty over your data:

  • Access & Export: You can export generated websites as complete ZIP archives directly from the Web Studio at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual chat threads, websites, or video projects directly from the dashboard. Upon deletion, records are immediately purged from our active Supabase PostgreSQL database.
  • Right to be Forgotten: To request complete eradication of your user profile and all associated data, email us at support@nova-ai.io.

7. Information Security

We enforce end-to-end industry security protocols:

  • All network transmissions are strictly enforced over HTTPS using TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Database connections use SSL with role-level separation; private API secrets are stored securely in backend serverless environments and never exposed to the client browser.
  • Session integrity is protected by timed, cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens.
